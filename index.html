About HackHeist

HackHeist is a developer-focused technology event designed to bring together students who are passionate about coding, innovation, and problem-solving. Through workshops, tech sessions, collaboration, and hands-on activities, participants can improve their technical skills, explore new ideas, and build innovative solutions.
